Entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mang-the-moon-finally-answers-back-781a7ec1): one line, 1 to 240 characters, in the voice of the Moon answering back at last to the wishes, the howls and the late-night promises of everyone staring up at it.

## The line

> You howl, you wish, you beg the sky — four billion years of it, and your best wish is still 'make him text back'? He's not going to. I can see him from up here.

## Why this line

- **Speaks as the Moon answering:** first-person Moon, replying directly to the crowd below — the howls, the wishes, the begging — after four billion years of listening.
- **Funny on one read:** the setup is the grandest wish in the sky; the punchline is the pettiest one, and the Moon, who sees everything from up there, already knows the answer.
- **Moon's own voice:** wry, old and a little tired of being wished upon — four billion years, no days off, and it has heard this exact wish before.
- **Lands as a comeback:** the turn arrives at the end — "He's not going to. I can see him from up here." — a verdict, not an explanation.
- **Tight:** 160 characters, three beats, nothing after the punchline.
